FixPin

Privacy

  • No accounts, no tracking, no cookies. The app and the extension have no analytics. The FixPin website counts visits anonymously, with no personal data.
  • What is stored: only what you choose to send: the screenshot, your note, the page address, your browser and screen size, and any page errors (extension only). It is stored under a random link that cannot be guessed.
  • How long: 1, 7 or 30 days, as you choose. After that it is deleted automatically. You can delete it earlier from the report page on the device that made it.
  • Passcode (optional): only a one-way hash is kept.
  • Who can see it: anyone with the link (and passcode, if set). Do not include passwords or card numbers in screenshots.
  • Your device: the app remembers your recent reports in this browser only, so you can find them again.
  • Open source: the code is public, so you can check all of this: fixpin.
