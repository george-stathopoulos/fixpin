# Privacy

- No accounts, no tracking, no cookies, no analytics.
- Stored: only what you send (screenshot, note, page address, browser and screen size, page errors from the extension), under a random 192-bit link.
- Kept 1, 7 or 30 days as you choose, then deleted automatically (Cloudflare KV expiry). The device that made a report can delete it earlier.
- A passcode is stored only as a salted SHA-256 hash.
- Anyone with the link (and passcode) can see the report. Do not include passwords or card numbers.
- The app remembers your recent reports in your browser only.
