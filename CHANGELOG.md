# Changelog

All notable changes to the FixPin app, website and browser extension, newest first.

## 2026-10-19

### Extension 0.1.2
- The box is now drawn while you drag, so you can see what you are selecting.
- Clicking or typing in the note panel no longer adds a pin. Picking has its own layer under the panel.
- The panel moves to the side of the page that has no marked spots.

### Extension 0.1.1
- Renamed to FixPin (from the working name FixSpot).

### App and website
- Renamed to **FixPin**. The working name was already taken on a browser extension store.
- The help center now has real screenshots and two animated illustrations (how it works, and the extension).
- The website home is one horizontal scene: scroll to move sideways, and the footer waits at the end. Your pointer is a flashlight, and the bugs hiding in the dark get boxed and numbered when the light finds them.
- A much brighter, wider flashlight.
- Links in the app use the accent colour.
- Visits to the website are counted anonymously, like the other project sites. The app and the extension have no analytics.

## 2026-10-18

### First release
- The app (installable PWA): add, drop, paste or share a screenshot; click or drag to mark up to 5 spots; add a note; choose 1, 7 or 30 days and an optional passcode; copy a private link.
- The report page: screenshot with numbered spots, details and errors, copy as text, delete early from the device that made it.
- The Cloudflare Worker: reports stored with automatic expiry, no accounts.
- The browser extension 0.1.0 for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox: capture the page, the spots, and page errors from the moment you click.
- Help center, privacy page and instructions to host your own.
