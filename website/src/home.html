How it works · 1 of 4

Capture it.

With the browser extension, click its button on the page with the problem. With the app, add, drop or paste a screenshot, or share one straight from your phone.

How it works · 2 of 4

Point at it.

Click the broken part, or drag a box around it. Up to five spots, each numbered. Add a note in your own words.

Submit
3
How it works · 3 of 4

Share the link.

Choose 1, 7 or 30 days and, if you like, a passcode. Copy the private link and send it. It carries the page, your screen, your browser and any errors.

How it works · 4 of 4

It cleans up after itself.

When the time is up, the report and its screenshot are deleted. You can also delete it any time. No account to close, nothing left behind.

SMALL
Small, on purpose

Everything you need to say “here”.

◎

Point, don't describe

Click or drag a box. Each spot is numbered, and the extension also records which page element it is on.

⚠

Errors come along

With the extension: JavaScript errors, failed requests and console warnings from the moment you click.

⏳

Links that expire

1, 7 or 30 days, then the report and screenshot are deleted. Delete earlier with one button.

⚿

Optional passcode

Ask for a passcode before the report opens. Only a one-way hash is stored.

▣

An installable app

Phone, tablet or computer. Add it to your home screen and share screenshots straight to it.

⧉

Copy the details

One button turns the report into plain text for an email or ticket: page, screen, browser, spots, errors.
